## How to read a rating

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ★★★★★ | Approved drug, RCT-confirmed efficacy, mature post-marketing safety data |
| ★★★★☆ | Approved (including narrow/accelerated) or human RCT evidence; long-term data incomplete |
| ★★★☆☆ | Human safety/PK or small uncontrolled trials; efficacy largely preclinical |
| ★★☆☆☆ | Preclinical only, plausible mechanism, no specific safety red flag |
| ★☆☆☆☆ | Experimental, contradictory, retracted, or a specific safety signal |

Evidence grades: **A** = randomised trials or approval; **B** = uncontrolled human trials/PK/case series; **C** = animal/preclinical only; **D** = in vitro or anecdotal.

A compound cannot exceed ★★★☆☆ on preclinical evidence alone.

## Standing caveats

1. **Enforcement discretion is not approval.** BPC-157, Epitalon, KPV, MOTS-c, Semax and TB-500 are covered by the Oct 2026 HHS policy for 503A compounding; they remain unapproved drugs and are not on the 503A bulks list.
2. **Purity is not identity, and identity is not fill.** Verify a COA at the testing lab's own portal (Janoshik, Freedom Diagnostics, Vanguard), not just on the vendor's site.
3. **Community signal is not evidence.** Reddit and forums are useful for logistics and batch problems; efficacy claims belong to the primary literature.
